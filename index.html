Making a 10-minute video with free credits

  1. Write a shot listSplit the video into chapters of 4 to 8 shots. Keep character and setting descriptions identical in every prompt.
  2. Spend credits on the hero shotsUse the daily free credits (Flow, Kling, Krea) for the few shots that need cinematic quality. Draft at low resolution first.
  3. Fill the rest with cheap formatsUse an avatar or script-to-video tool for narration sections, and image-to-video to keep characters consistent between clips.
  4. Assemble in an editorStitch chapters in CapCut, Canva or Kapwing. Daily credit refills mean a long project can run over several days.
